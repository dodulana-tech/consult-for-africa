Belfiore · The view from the top

Attributed · About 6 minutes

This survey is attributed. This one is attributed, because the value is in seeing where the senior team agrees and where it does not. Answer as you believe things actually are today, not as you intend them to be.
For most questions, choose how much you agree from 1 to 5. Every question also has N/A · Do not know, and you should use it freely where a question does not apply to you.

01What good looks like here

1.We have a written standard for how clients are to be handled.
2.Every client-facing person could tell you what that standard is.
3.Our team has been properly trained for the job we actually ask them to do.
4.Our team has the authority to resolve a small problem without coming to me.
5.Our team represents Belfiore the way I would represent it myself.

02Doing the job day to day

6.I know how quickly our enquiries are answered.
7.I know what proportion of enquiries turn into booked appointments.
8.The team is sized correctly for the volume of enquiries we get.
9.Our systems let the team do the job well rather than getting in the way.
10.I would be comfortable listening back to any call we took this week.

03When it is difficult

11.Complaints reach me reliably rather than by accident.
12.We have a method for handling a client who is unhappy with a result.
13.Every complaint is recorded somewhere I could review it.
14.We would handle a public complaint on social media well.

04The client who is not in the room

15.We follow up with every client after their treatment.
16.We know which clients are due to return, and we act on it.
17.We systematically get back in touch with clients we have not seen in a while.
18.I know how much of our revenue comes from returning clients.

05How we know it is working

19.We measure client experience in a way I actually trust.
20.Client experience is a standing item at our management meetings.
21.Somebody independently checks how our enquiries are handled.
22.One named person owns client experience at Belfiore.
23.Managers coach the team on client handling, rather than only correcting mistakes.
