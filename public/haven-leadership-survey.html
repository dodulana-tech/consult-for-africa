Founders' Direction Survey

A short, confidential read on where each founder's instincts sit. It should take about ten minutes. There are no right answers, only trade-offs.

Please complete this on your own, without conferring. This is not a satisfaction survey. It forces choices between good things you cannot have in full at once, and it asks what you think the others believe. The value is in an honest, individual picture, so the moment you compare notes beforehand, the most useful finding disappears. Your individual answers are seen only by Consult for Africa and brought back to the board as one combined picture.
