01What good looks like here

1.I know exactly what Belfiore expects of me when a client gets in touch.
2.There is a written standard for how quickly we reply to an enquiry.
3.When I am not sure what to do, I know who to ask.
4.I am clear about what I can decide myself and what I have to pass up.
5.I can confidently answer most questions a client asks me about our treatments.
6.I can quote a price to a client without checking with somebody else.

02Doing the job day to day

7.I have enough time in the day to handle enquiries properly.
8.The number of enquiries I handle in a day is manageable.
9.When a client contacts us I can find their history quickly.
10.The phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, the diary and our records work well together.
11.Nothing falls through the cracks when a client moves from one channel to another.
12.Clients are rarely kept waiting longer than they were told to expect.
13.It is hard to keep a conversation private at the front desk.
14.I am interrupted so often that I lose track of what I was doing.
15.I spend time on tasks that somebody else, or a system, should be doing.

03When it is difficult

16.I feel confident handling a client who is angry or upset.

18.I am clear about what I must not say about a clinical outcome.
19.I would know what to do if a client threatened to post about us online.
20.When an interaction becomes difficult, a senior colleague steps in and supports me.
21.There is a clear way to record a complaint so that it is not forgotten.

04The client who is not in the room

23.We reliably check in with a client after their treatment.

27.We get back in touch with clients we have not seen for a long time.
28.Clients usually leave with their next appointment already booked.

29.We ask clients for feedback in an organised way.
