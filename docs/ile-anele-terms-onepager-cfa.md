## An invitation to build ilé

Dr Anele, ilé is a new healthcare venture whose core is home services and residential eldercare, built to a clinical standard this market rarely sees. We are inviting you to join as a founding clinical partner: the clinical conscience of the business, setting standards and leading care. This page sets out the proposed terms, in plain language, as a basis for conversation.

### Your role

Founding clinical partner and clinical lead. You own clinical standards, protocols and governance. You are a founder, not an employee, and you are at the table on the decisions that matter.

### Your equity

A **10% founder stake** in ilé, granted for the clinical leadership you bring rather than for cash. This is your founder grant, and it is capped at 10%, but you also get a real, rewarded route to more, below.

### How it vests

Founder equity is earned by building, so the 10% vests rather than landing all at once:

- **A one-year cliff.** Stay and build for the first year and a quarter of your stake vests. This protects everyone if the fit turns out to be wrong early.
- **Four-year vesting.** The rest vests month by month over the following three years, so the full 10% is yours after four years.
- You hold the shares from day one. Vesting simply means the company can buy back the unvested part at nominal value if you leave early, and if you leave for reasons outside your control you keep what has vested.

### Growing your stake

At each future fundraise, you get a personal right to buy more ilé shares at a **meaningful discount** to the price others pay, up to an agreed ceiling (we suggest growing you toward 15% over time). It is a right, not an obligation, and it rewards you for staying and building.

### What we ask of you

- Clinical leadership, standards and governance, and the time commitment we agree.
- That your clinical protocols and contributions are assigned to ilé, as everyone's contributions are, so the business owns what makes it work.
- Good-faith commitment, reflected in fair leaver terms that protect you and the venture alike.

### What happens next

We put these terms into a founders' agreement alongside Kemi (through her holding company) and Consult for Africa, who build the technology and manage the operation. Please take your own independent legal and tax advice on it, then let us sit down and build something worth being proud of.
