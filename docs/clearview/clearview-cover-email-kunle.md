Subject: Clearview, some thoughts after our conversation

Hi Kunle,

Thank you for the conversation, and for being as candid as you were about where Clearview is after nine years. Most people in your position describe the business they wish they had. You described the one you actually have, which is a good deal more useful to me.

I have put some thoughts together, and there are two documents attached. The first is a short strategy note. The second is a discovery questionnaire, which is the list of things I would need to know before any of the first one becomes a plan. I want to be clear that neither is a proposal, because nothing at Clearview has been counted yet and I would rather be useful to you than confident in front of you.

A few things I want to say plainly up front.

The first is that you told me you believe in systems and do not think you have one. I would put it differently. You have a system and it has worked for nine years, which is longer than most fertility units in this country manage. The problem is that the system is you, so it cannot grow past your own clinic hours and it is worth considerably less to anyone else than it is to you. Stability and sustainability, which is what you said you were after, are really another way of describing a business that still works in a week when you are not in it.

The second is about the marketing funnel, and this is where I may be about to disagree with you. In a fertility business the money is almost never lost between the advertisement and the enquiry. It is lost between the enquiry and the first cycle, while somebody who has already decided she wants a child is working out whether she can afford one and whether she trusts you. Advertising before that gap is closed just pours more people into the same leak, and your cost per cycle goes up while your margin goes down.

The third is the one I would most like to talk to you about, and it is the reason the note is longer than I intended. I think Clearview is being run as a Lekki clinic serving Lekki. Lekki is the most contested and most expensive fertility catchment in West Africa, and competing there on fit out and advertising is the one fight where none of your real advantages count for anything. But Lekki is an excellent destination. The catchment that should matter to you is mainland Lagos, the rest of Nigeria, West Africa and the diaspora, which is to say everyone who cannot get this done properly at home. Four extra cycles a month from outside Lagos is somewhere between two hundred and sixty and three hundred and sixty five million naira a year, on a laboratory you have already built and already paid for.

The fourth is why I think that is genuinely available to you rather than to anybody else. A woman who travels for IVF has two problems nobody in Lagos solves. She cannot live here for three weeks while she is monitored, and she is frightened of becoming unwell a long way from home with a doctor who does not know what was done to her. Ovarian hyperstimulation, as you know far better than I do, tends to declare itself several days after retrieval, which is exactly when she has flown home. You own a hospital. You are the only fertility operator in Lekki who can tell her that if anything goes wrong she will be admitted by your own team, and that it is already in the price. None of the clinics you compete with can say that without buying a hospital first.

There is one more thing in the note that you may not like, and I would rather flag it now than have you find it on page eight. I think the group brand should lead and your own name should sit behind it, which is the opposite of what I would normally advise a specialist. My reasons are in section 8 and I am happy to be argued out of them.

The questionnaire is long because you have two businesses and I would rather ask everything once. Please do not spend more than an hour on it, and please do not go and calculate anything. Where you do not track something, just say so, because in this field that is usually the most useful answer.

The natural first step is a conversation about sections 4, 6 and 8. Bring your cycle register and your last twelve months. If the leaks are where I suspect they are, you will have found the money before you have spent any, and if they are not then I have been usefully wrong early and we will both know what the real question is.
