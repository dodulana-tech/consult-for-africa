# Haven Paediatric Centre — Diagnostic Audit
## Information & Data Request

Consult for Africa · Workstream 1 (Diagnostic Audit) · Prepared for the Haven Paediatric Centre board and management

**Point of contact (Haven):** to be nominated (we suggest the Head of Operations)
**How to share:** a single secure, read-only shared folder (Google Drive / OneDrive) with sub-folders per section below. Where a live system export is easier (accounting, EMR, payroll), a CSV/Excel export is fine. Mark anything commercially sensitive; it is handled under strict confidentiality and used only for this audit.

> **The 12 Week-1 Critical items are flagged ⚑.** These unlock the analysis immediately; the rest can follow during weeks 1–2. Nothing here is a test — partial or imperfect records are themselves a finding, so please share what exists rather than reconstructing anything.

---

## A. Corporate, licensing & governance
- Certificate of incorporation, shareholding/ownership structure, and current board composition
- Facility operating licence and regulatory registrations (HEFAMAA Lagos and any others) with current validity
- Organisation chart / organogram with reporting lines ⚑
- Register of current policies and the SOP index (list of what exists)
- Board and management meeting minutes for the last 12 months
- Any professional indemnity / facility insurance policies, and details of any pending litigation or regulatory action

## B. Finance — statements, ledgers & cash
- Monthly management accounts / profit & loss for the last 12 months ⚑
- Latest balance sheet and most recent prior year-end
- Bank statements for **all** accounts, last 6–12 months ⚑
- Latest trial balance and the chart of accounts
- Current-year budget and budget-vs-actual, if prepared
- Fixed asset register
- Any audited financials or prior accountant's/auditor's reports
- Name and access (or exports) for the accounting system in use

## C. Revenue & receivables
- Revenue by service line / department, monthly, last 12 months ⚑
- Revenue split by payer: self-pay/private vs each HMO
- Debtors / receivables ageing by payer, current ⚑ (this is where the ~N4.2M is sitting)
- Current price list / tariff for all services ⚑
- Deposit, billing and credit policy (documented or described)

## D. HMO / payer economics
- All HMO contracts and tariff schedules (Leadway, NEM, and any others) ⚑
- HMO claims register: submitted / approved / rejected / paid / outstanding, per HMO
- Claim rejection reasons (a summary or the raw log)
- Enrollee volumes and typical claim-cycle time per HMO
- Any capitation vs fee-for-service arrangements

## E. Pharmacy, stock & inventory
- Current full stock count / inventory listing — drugs and consumables — with quantities **and** values ⚑ (this is the ~N2.77M)
- Stock valuation method and the last stock-count date
- Pharmacy sales report, last 6–12 months
- Purchase records / supplier invoices, last 6–12 months
- Reorder levels and stock-control policy, if any
- Expired / near-expiry and wastage log
- Stockout log or list of known stockout incidents
- Current crash-cart contents list and any existing check records
- Pharmacy margin working (cost price vs selling price by line, if available)

## F. Procurement & payables
- Supplier list with credit terms and typical lead times
- Procurement and approval process (documented or described)
- Creditors / accounts-payable ageing
- Any existing vendor-managed-inventory or consignment arrangements

## G. Staffing, payroll & HR
- Full staff list: name, role, department, employment type (full-time / part-time / locum), start date ⚑
- Payroll register / salary schedule — gross, allowances, deductions, net — last 3 months ⚑
- Job descriptions for all roles, and any current KPIs
- Current staff commission / incentive structure, and the proposed one awaiting board approval ⚑
- Employment contracts / offer letters (samples plus key clinical roles)
- Duty rosters / shift schedules, last 2 months
- Staff turnover: joiners and leavers, last 12 months
- Training records (including BLS / PALS / NRP / resuscitation)
- Disciplinary and grievance log
- Statutory compliance status: PAYE, pension, NHF

## H. Clinical governance & patient safety
- Clinical protocols and guidelines in use (general paediatrics and NICU)
- Incident / adverse-event register and near-miss log
- Mortality & morbidity review records and M&M meeting minutes
- Emergency / resuscitation protocols, the crash-cart standard, and resuscitation training records
- Medication-management policy and sample drug charts
- Infection prevention & control policy and any audit records
- NICU admission criteria and protocols
- Consent forms and sample clinical documentation
- Patient complaints register

## I. Operations & patient flow
- Patient volumes by month, last 12 months: OPD attendances, admissions, NICU admissions ⚑
- Average length of stay and bed occupancy (general and NICU)
- Booking / appointment system and its data
- SOPs for registration, triage, admission, discharge and billing (what exists)
- Any patient-satisfaction or customer-feedback data
- Facility layout and bed configuration

## J. Management reporting & systems
- The current management report(s) — including the fortnightly report — and any dashboards ⚑
- The list of KPIs currently tracked
- The EMR / HMIS / billing software in use, with read access or data exports

---

## What happens with this
Week 1, we reconcile the numbers, size the working-capital opportunity precisely, and map the operating model. Week 2 we go on site for interviews and observation. Week 3 is finance, procurement, HMO and reporting deep-dives. Week 4 we synthesise into a prioritised, costed plan and present to the board. Two quick wins — the crash-cart standard and the Leadway/NEM receivables push — start in week 1, before the audit reports.
