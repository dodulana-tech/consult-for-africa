Anything in [square brackets] is a placeholder for a fact or number to confirm, most of which the discovery questionnaire will supply. ---

## 1. What the site has to do

The current site leads with a generic "care you can trust, every step of the way," splits patients into Nigeria and international, mentions robotic knee, and buries the one thing that is actually the moat: the complex, revision and infection work. It has no visible outcomes, no proof, no testimonials, and no pathway for the referring doctors who are the real engine of this practice.

The rebuild has four jobs, in order:

1. **Claim the complex-case authority in the first screen.** A visitor should understand within five seconds that this is the surgeon for the hard cases, revision, infection, complex trauma, and the cases that went wrong elsewhere.
2. **Convert the two real buyers:** the patient (or their diaspora child paying), and the referring doctor. These need different pathways, and the site currently serves neither well.
3. **Prove it.** Outcomes, volumes, credentials, and real patient stories, shown not asserted. This is what closes the gap against a competitor who leads with "4,000+ procedures."
4. **Carry the honest-counsel voice throughout,** so the whole site feels like a surgeon who tells the truth, including when not to operate.

**What we are deliberately not doing:** competing head-on with "World-Class Orthopedics, Here at Home." That flag is taken. We lead with the complex-case authority and let world-class-care-at-home be the closing reassurance, attached to the hard cases.

## 2. Site architecture

A tight, purposeful set of pages. Depth where it earns trust, nothing decorative.

| Page | Job | Primary audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Home** | Claim the position, route the two buyers, show proof fast | All |
| **Complex & Revision Surgery** | The hero clinical page: revision, infection, failed/foreign surgery, limb reconstruction | Patients + referrers |
| **Hip & Knee Replacement** | Primary elective, reassurance, what to expect | Patients |
| **About Dr Bola** | The credentials, the story, the ethics-forward voice | All |
| **Patient Stories & Outcomes** | Proof: testimonials, before/after, numbers | Patients |
| **For Referring Doctors** | The public face of the portal: the referral pathway, promise, MDT and fellowship, with a login | Doctors |
| **Clinical Community Portal** (gated) | Doctor-only login: refer and track, join MDTs, learn, become a fellow | Doctors |
| **For Patients Abroad / Diaspora** | The recapture and diaspora-funded pathway | Diaspora |
| **Plan Your Care** | The single conversion hub: how to book, what it costs, what happens next | Patients |
| **Insights** (light) | A few authority pieces for SEO and thought leadership | All + search |
| **Contact** | Osteon location, visiting sites, WhatsApp, secure enquiry | All |

Global calls to action, present on every page: **"Book a consultation"** (patient) and a quieter **"Refer a patient"** / **"Doctor login"** (doctor).

**The Clinical Community Portal is a major, separate workstream, not just a page.** It is the doctor-only, login-gated platform where doctors refer and track patients, join complex-case MDTs, learn from Dr Bola, and progress to the OrthoSurplus fellowship. The public "For Referring Doctors" page is its shop window; the portal itself is the product. It is specified in full in the companion document, *The Clinical Community Portal*, and should be built in phases (referral MVP first). The website and the portal share one login and one design system.

## 3. Voice and design direction

**Voice.** Senior, calm, precise, humane. The register of a consultant who has seen the hard cases and does not need to boast. Short sentences. Plain English over jargon, with the medical term in brackets where it reassures. Honesty as a feature: he will tell you when surgery is not the answer. No hype, no superlatives stacked on superlatives, and no em dashes anywhere in the copy.

**Design.** Premium, clinical, uncluttered. White space, one strong accent, real photography of him and the work (not stock). Legible at speed. Trust signals visible without scrolling: FRCS, the fellowships, the institutions. Mobile-first, because most Nigerian traffic and most diaspora browsing is on a phone. Fast: this must load quickly on a mid-tier Android on Nigerian mobile data.

**Proof furniture** that should recur across pages: credential row (FRCS, BOA fellowship, MPH, years, countries), a live outcomes strip [numbers to confirm], and at least one real patient quote.

### 4.2 Complex & Revision Surgery (the hero clinical page)

> **Headline:** Complex, revision and salvage surgery
>
> **Intro:** Some cases are harder than others. A joint replacement that has worn out, loosened or become infected. A fracture that has not united. A limb that needs reconstruction after serious injury. An operation done elsewhere, sometimes abroad, that has not gone to plan. These cases need a surgeon who does them often, not occasionally. That is the core of my practice.

**What I treat**

> - **Revision hip and knee replacement.** Redoing a joint replacement that has failed, loosened, worn or dislocated.
> - **Bone and joint infection.** Diagnosing and clearing infection in and around joints and implants, one of the most demanding areas in orthopaedics.
> - **Failed or complicated surgery from elsewhere.** Including cases operated on abroad that have returned with a problem. It is often possible to put these right.
> - **Complex trauma and limb reconstruction.** Rebuilding after severe fractures, non-union and deformity.

**Why this is specialist work**

> Revision and infection surgery is not a bigger version of a routine replacement. It is a different discipline: harder to plan, harder to execute, and far less forgiving. It rewards a surgeon who has done a great many of them and who works to an international standard. That is what I offer, and it is why other doctors refer their most difficult cases here.

**A note on second opinions**

> If you have been told that nothing more can be done, or that your only option is to travel abroad, it is worth a second opinion before you decide. Many complex cases are more treatable than they first appear.

## 5. Proof and trust (build these in, do not bolt on)

- **Real outcomes,** even if simple to begin with: numbers of replacements and revisions, years, complication or revision rates if he tracks them. Confirmed and kept honest. This is the single most persuasive element and the direct answer to "4,000+ procedures."
- **Real patient stories,** with consent: the person, the problem, the result. One strong story beats ten adjectives.
- **Credentials, always visible:** FRCS, BOA fellowship, MPH, the institutions, the three countries.
- **Named affiliations:** Osteon, Cedarcrest, Diamed, shown as where care happens.
- **Google Business profile** claimed, complete, and gathering reviews. Most patients will check.

## 6. Search and discovery (SEO essentials)

Target the phrases patients and their families actually type. Own the ground the competitor is weak on: the complex and revision terms.

- **Priority terms:** "revision knee replacement Lagos", "revision hip replacement Nigeria", "infected joint replacement Nigeria", "failed knee replacement Nigeria", "second opinion joint replacement Lagos", "hip replacement Lagos", "knee replacement Lagos cost", "orthopaedic surgeon Lagos hip knee".
- Each service page targets a cluster of these in real, plain content, not keyword stuffing.
- **Insights pieces** (3 to 5 to start) built around real patient questions: "Do I really need a knee replacement?", "What to do when a joint replacement fails", "Is it safe to have your joint replacement in Nigeria?", "Signs your hip replacement may be infected". These earn search traffic and carry the honest-counsel voice.
- Fast load, clean mobile, proper page titles and descriptions, local business schema. The basics, done properly, will out-rank most of the field.

## 7. Build notes for designer and developer

- Mobile-first, fast on Nigerian mobile data, tested on mid-tier Android.
- WhatsApp as a first-class contact channel, not an afterthought.
- A working referral form and a working booking/enquiry form, going somewhere that is actually monitored, with a same-week response.
- Analytics from day one, so we can see what converts.
- Photography: a proper shoot of Dr Bola and of Osteon. This alone lifts the brand above most competitors.
- Keep the two-brand rule visible in every layout: **Dr Bola Akinola** is the masthead; **Osteon Clinics** appears as the place of care, endorsed, never dominant.
