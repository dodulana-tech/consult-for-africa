# Lyfe Place Abuja: architectural brief for the target operating state

A complete description of what the building must become. Written to be handed to an
architect, or pasted whole into a drawing tool, without further explanation.

Areas are measured from the supplied as-built PDFs and are approximate. **Confirm against
the CAD before drawing.**

---

## 1. The commission

Convert an existing four-structure residential compound in Abuja into a private ambulatory
medical campus. **No structural extension. No lift. No operating theatre.** The building is
sound and needs interior decoration, new partitions and a services upgrade, not a strip-out.

Produce a drawing set for the target operating state described below.

## 6. Circulation and flow

Draw these four flows and keep them from crossing where noted.

| Flow | Route |
|---|---|
| **Patient** | Car park, single entrance, reception, waiting zone, called to room, out past the medication collection point |
| **Staff** | Separate back-of-house route to the lounge, sterilising and linen without passing through waiting |
| **Specimen** | Draw points on both floors, to the chalet laboratory. **Must not cross the patient waiting area** |
| **Clinical waste** | From every clinical room to an external holding area with its own hardstanding and vehicle access. **Must not cross the patient or specimen routes** |

## 7. Services, campus-wide

| Package | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Cooling** | About 28 split units. Ceiling cassettes in reception, waiting and the lounge; wall units elsewhere. No central plant |
| **Ventilation** | Mechanical fresh air and extract to all clinical rooms. Splits recirculate and do not ventilate. Modest positive pressure to the clean procedure room only |
| **Electrical** | New distribution board and load zoning for a 25KVA hybrid solar system with grid and generator changeover. Dedicated medical circuits to imaging, laboratory and procedure rooms. Emergency lighting throughout |
| **ICT** | Structured cabling, CCTV, access control, room-status displays. **No nurse call system.** Hardwired emergency call buttons in every clinical room and emergency pull cords in every WC. All other operational calling is handled in software |
| **Fire** | Detection, alarm, escape lighting, extinguishers, compartmentation to the staircase |
| **Water** | Borehole, treatment, storage, pressurisation, hot water to all clinical hand-wash points |
| **Flooring** | Welded and coved clinical vinyl to all clinical rooms and the laboratory. Quality finishes front of house |

## 8. Hard constraints

These are not preferences. A drawing that breaks any of them is wrong.

1. **No lift, and none is to be added.** No sedation-dependent work above ground level, at any budget. A sedated patient must be evacuated horizontally on a trolley.
2. **One reception only.** Do not draw a second reception or a second entrance for patients. The single desk sustains seven to eight arrivals an hour across a 07:00 to 21:00 window, and that is the binding constraint on the whole campus.
3. **X-ray stays on the ground floor.** Weight, lead shielding and evacuation.
4. **No operating theatre.** Positive-pressure HEPA ventilation, isolated power and monitored recovery cannot be accommodated. If day-case surgery is ever wanted it goes in a modular unit on the grounds, drawn separately.
5. **No inpatient beds.** This is an ambulatory facility. Recovery bays are for the same day.

## 9. Drawing set required

1. Floor plans, all four structures, 1:50, with new partitions distinguished from retained walls
2. Reflected ceiling plans with lighting and cassette positions
3. Services layouts: electrical and data, mechanical, fire
4. Room data sheets for every clinical room: finishes, services, equipment, hand-wash
5. Flow diagram showing the four routes in section 6
6. Furniture and equipment layout for reception, waiting and the two procedure rooms
7. External works: car park, drop-off, waste holding, chalet and BQ approaches
8. Signage and wayfinding schedule

the scheme is wrong rather than the number.
