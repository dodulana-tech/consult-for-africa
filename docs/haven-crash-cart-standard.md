# Haven Paediatric Centre — Crash-Cart Standard & Shift Checklist

Week-1 quick win, live before the audit reports. A stocked, sealed, checklist-governed paediatric crash cart with a routine that runs every shift, by habit, not reminder. Finalise contents and doses with Dr Shakirah and Dr Odedina against Haven's protocols and patient mix; this is the operating standard, not a clinical prescription.

---

## The standard
1. **One cart, one standard layout.** Same drawer for the same thing, every time. A laminated contents map on the cart.
2. **Sealed when ready.** A numbered breakaway seal. Intact seal = cart is complete and in date. Broken seal = must be restocked, re-checked and re-sealed before it counts as ready.
3. **Checked every shift.** At the start of each shift the responsible nurse confirms the seal is intact and the seal number matches the log. A full contents-and-expiry check at least daily and after every use.
4. **Restock immediately after any use.** The cart is returned to full, in-date, and re-sealed before the shift ends — never "later."
5. **Owned.** A named role (matron / shift lead) owns cart readiness each shift and signs the log.

## Contents checklist (confirm/adjust with clinical leads)
**Airway / breathing**
- [ ] Paediatric & neonatal bag-valve-mask; masks (sizes)
- [ ] Oxygen source + tubing; suction + catheters (sizes)
- [ ] Oropharyngeal airways (range); ET tubes (range) + introducer; laryngoscope + blades (working, spare batteries)

**Circulation / access**
- [ ] IV cannulae (paediatric/neonatal range); IO access device
- [ ] Syringes, needles, giving sets; fluids (as per protocol)
- [ ] Tape, tourniquet, gloves

**Emergency drugs (doses per Haven paediatric/neonatal protocol)**
- [ ] Adrenaline / epinephrine
- [ ] Atropine
- [ ] Dextrose (appropriate concentration)
- [ ] Adenosine
- [ ] Amiodarone
- [ ] Naloxone
- [ ] Hydrocortisone
- [ ] Salbutamol / bronchodilator
- [ ] Anticonvulsant (per protocol)
- [ ] Sodium bicarbonate (per protocol)
- [ ] Normal saline / flushes

**Monitoring / equipment**
- [ ] Pulse oximeter (paediatric/neonatal probe)
- [ ] Glucometer + strips
- [ ] BP cuffs (sizes); thermometer
- [ ] Defibrillator with paediatric pads/paddles (if applicable), checked & charged

**Documentation**
- [ ] Paediatric resuscitation algorithm / weight-dose chart on the cart
- [ ] Broselow-style weight/dose reference (if used)
- [ ] Incident/resuscitation record forms

---

*This standard, plus the shift log, is the first thing we put in place. It costs almost nothing and it is the most visible signal to the team that the way things are done is changing.*
