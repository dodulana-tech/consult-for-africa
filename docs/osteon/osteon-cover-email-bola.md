Dear Bola,

Thank you for making Thursday work at short notice, and for being open to us looking at the whole business rather than only the parts that face the patient. I know that is not a comfortable thing to invite, and I do not take it lightly.

Attached are two documents for you and one set of forms.

The first document is the scope, which explains what we are looking at and why, how the day on Thursday will run, and what you get at the end of it. The second is the information and data request, which is long on purpose because the point of this exercise is to see the organisation end to end. Please do not let its length worry anybody, because nothing in it is a test, and where a record does not exist the right answer is to tell us it does not exist rather than to build one for us over the next two days.

A few things I want to say plainly up front.

The first is that the request has a short list at the front of nine items, and those nine are the ones that matter before Thursday. If the week gets away from your team, send those nine and let the rest follow over the fortnight after. Without them the visit becomes a tour, and I would rather it were an audit.

The second is that I expect the headline finding to be about the boundary between your practice and your company rather than about Amuwo Odofin. In July you told me the location was the constraint, and I said then that I did not think it was. What I think we will find is that a meaningful share of what you earn is earned in somebody else's theatre, and that Osteon carries a fixed cost base for a surgeon who is often somewhere else, which is a solvable problem but a different one from the postcode. I may be wrong, and the numbers will settle it either way, but I would rather tell you now what I am expecting to see than surprise you with it in a report.

The third is about sterile processing and implant traceability, which we will spend real time on because arthroplasty is the one specialty where getting those two wrong does not produce a small problem. I am not raising it because I have any reason to think there is anything wrong, and I am raising it now so that nobody on your team feels ambushed on the day when we ask to see autoclave cycle logs and lot stickers.

The fourth is the four surveys, which go to your staff, your patients, the doctors who refer to you, and to you and your senior people. The staff and patient ones are anonymous and their answers come to us rather than to you, which is the only way they are worth running. The referrer survey is the one I care most about, because the positioning we agreed in July depends on a referral pipeline and, so far as I know, nobody has ever asked those doctors directly what they actually want from you. For that one I need a list of names and numbers from you, including the people who used to refer and stopped, because what they say will be more useful than what your current referrers say.

What I need from you before Thursday is one point of contact with the authority to chase people, the nine priority items, a short note to your team from you saying that we are coming and that this is not about individuals, and the referrer list. A note in your own name lands very differently from one in mine.
