**A note for Dr Bola to send to the Osteon team, in his own name.**
WhatsApp group or printed for the noticeboard, ideally on Tuesday or Wednesday so nobody
meets the team cold on Thursday. Keep it as his words. If he wants to shorten it, the two
paragraphs that must survive are the one about nobody being in trouble and the one about
showing them the real version.

---

Good afternoon everyone.

On Thursday we will have visitors. A firm called Consult for Africa is spending the day with us, and they will be back over the following week. I have asked them to come, and I want to tell you myself what they are here for before anybody hears a rumour.

I want to grow this practice. To do that properly I need an honest picture of how Osteon actually works day to day, and I am not the right person to produce it, because I am in theatre or at another hospital for a lot of the week and I only see parts of it. So I have asked people from outside to look at the whole thing: the clinic, the theatre, the sterile room, the store, the records, the money, all of it.

Nobody is in trouble. This is not an investigation and it is not about any individual. I have not asked them to look into anybody and they will not be reporting to me on who said what. If they find something that is not working, that is almost always because of how we have set the place up, and setting the place up is my job, not yours.

The one thing I would ask of you is this. Please show them the real version. If a form does not get filled in when we are busy, let them see that. If something is broken, or you have been managing without it for months, tell them. If there is a rule here that nobody follows because it does not work, say so. A tidy version of Osteon is no use to me, because I cannot fix a problem that has been hidden for the day.

There is also a short survey, and it is anonymous. Your answers go to Consult for Africa, not to me, and I will only ever see grouped totals with no names attached. Please be honest in it, including about me and about how this place is managed. That is exactly what I am paying for.

They will want to spend a little time with most of you, around thirty minutes, and they will be walking around the building and looking at records. Please give them whatever access they ask for. If anything makes you uncomfortable, come to me directly.

Thank you for the work you do here. I know it is not always easy, and this is part of how I intend to make it better.

Dr Bola Akinola
