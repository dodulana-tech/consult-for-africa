# Havana Specialist Hospital, Board and Committee Enablement

## A proposal from Consult for Africa

**Prepared for:** The Board of Havana Specialist Hospital Limited
**Prepared by:** Dr Debo Odulana, Founding Partner, Consult for Africa
**Date:** 2 July 2026
**Classification:** Confidential

> Following the board's decision to adopt the operating model, this proposal covers the two things needed to make it work in practice: building the board and committee instruments (templates, packs, calendars), and training the executive directors and management to run board and committee interactions well, from reporting and template use to chairing the committees themselves.

---

## 1. Where this sits

The board has agreed the shape: a two-tier committee architecture, five board committees, a clear relationship with the Afya-HSH Governance Committee, reporting pathways, and a delegation-of-authority schedule. That is the design. Two things now turn the design into a working board.

First, the **instruments**: the templates, packs, and calendars that make reporting consistent and the committees run to a standard, so the Company Secretary has a system to administer rather than a set of principles to interpret.

Second, the **capability**: the executive directors and management need to know how to operate inside this model, how to report by exception, how to use the templates, how to chair a committee, and how information should flow between management, the Governance Committee, the committees, and the board.

This proposal delivers both, and offers optional support through the first governance cycle so the model beds in rather than drifts.

---

## 2. Objectives

- Give HSH a complete, ready-to-use set of board and committee instruments, aligned to the adopted operating model and the delegation-of-authority schedule.
- Equip the executive directors and management to run board and committee interactions with confidence: reporting, template use, meeting discipline, and chairing.
- Establish the reporting and escalation habits that keep the board informed by exception and the committees effective.
- Embed the fair-process and conflict discipline that keeps a two-family board legitimate and functioning.
- Leave behind a self-sustaining system the Company Secretary administers without ongoing dependence.

---

## 3. Scope

### Workstream 1, Board and committee instruments

Development of the full template and document suite, built on the operating model already adopted and finalised with the Company Secretary.

- **Board pack template**, a single consolidated pack: agenda, chair's notes, management report, committee reports, risk summary, finance pack, decisions log.
- **Committee report templates**, one-page reporting templates for each of the five board committees and for the Afya-HSH Governance Committee, on a common format (metrics against target, exceptions, risks, decisions requested).
- **Agenda, minutes, and action-log templates**, standardised across the board and committees.
- **Reporting dashboard and KPI pack**, the standing management and performance dashboard that feeds the board by exception.
- **Board and committee annual calendar**, a rolling twelve-month schedule sequencing committees to feed the board in the right order.
- **Delegation-of-authority schedule, finalised**, thresholds set with the board and operationalised.
- **Committee terms of reference, finalised**, from the drafts already prepared.

### Workstream 2, Capability and training

Practical training for the executive directors and management on running board and committee interactions, delivered as working sessions rather than lectures.

- **The board and management operating model**, the distinction between governing and managing, and what each committee is for.
- **Reporting and escalation discipline**, how to report by exception, what escalates and when, and how to use the templates so the board gets decision-ready information.
- **Using the instruments**, hands-on practice with the board pack, committee reports, dashboards, and action logs.
- **Chairing a committee**, running a committee meeting, managing time and debate, reaching and recording decisions, and handling conflicts and recusals.
- **Board interaction and fair process**, how information flows between management, the Governance Committee, the committees, and the board, and how to keep deliberation fair and inclusive in a two-family board.
- **The Afya interface**, how management and the Governance Committee report upward and keep the board's independent line of sight intact.
- **A Board and Committee Handbook**, a concise reference the directors and Company Secretary keep and reuse.

### Optional, Supported first governance cycle

Consult for Africa attends and quietly coaches through the first quarter of board and committee meetings, refining the templates against real use and giving each committee chair light, private feedback, so the model is embedded, not just launched.

---

## 4. Approach

- **Built on what is adopted.** Every template and session derives from the operating model, terms of reference, and delegation schedule already prepared, so nothing is generic or off-the-shelf.
- **Done with the Company Secretary, not around them.** The Company Secretary is the long-term owner and is involved throughout, so administration transfers cleanly.
- **Practical and hands-on.** Training uses HSH's own templates and real agenda items, so directors practise on the actual system they will run.
- **Fair-process aware.** The training carries the governance discipline the board needs, conflicts, recusal, inclusive deliberation, not just mechanics.

---

## 5. Sustaining the model: the board secretariat and a governance owner

The instruments and training create the system; someone must own and run it after we step back. The board secretariat, maintaining the templates and packs, the calendar, minutes and action logs, the conflicts and related-party register, the delegation-of-authority schedule, and committee coordination, is a standing professional function, not a task to be shared informally among directors. We recommend HSH give it a dedicated owner, and set out two routes.

**Option A, secondment from the Company Secretary (recommended first step).** The board secretariat properly belongs with the Company Secretary, who already holds the statutory company-secretarial role and the governance expertise. The most direct route is to request a secondment, or a named dedicated resource, from them to run the secretariat day to day. It is fast, low-commitment, and keeps the secretariat where it belongs while the model beds in.

**Option B, an in-house legal counsel or governance professional.** As HSH scales, a permanent in-house counsel gives the hospital its own dedicated owner of board administration, compliance, contracts (including the Afya agreement), related-party and conflicts management, and regulatory matters. This role absorbs the secretariat and becomes the hospital's standing governance and legal capability.

**Responsibilities, either route.**
- Assemble and circulate the board pack, and manage the annual calendar.
- Keep minutes, decisions, and the action log across the board and all committees.
- Maintain the conflicts and related-party register, and administer the delegation-of-authority schedule.
- Coordinate the committees and the interface with the Afya-HSH Governance Committee.
- Support the chairs and hold reporting to the agreed templates.

**Option C, a legal practitioner seconded by Consult for Africa.** As an immediate, affordable bridge, Consult for Africa can second a qualified legal practitioner to carry the in-house counsel and board-secretariat function on a modest quarterly retainer. This gives HSH a professional governance owner from day one, at a fraction of a permanent salary, while it decides on a longer-term arrangement. Terms are in Section 8.

**Recommendation.** Stand the secretariat up now rather than leave it to directors. Either request a secondment from the Company Secretary, or take the Consult for Africa secondment as an affordable bridge, and plan toward a permanent in-house counsel as the hospital grows. Consult for Africa can also help define the role and, through CadreHealth, source and vet a permanent candidate when HSH is ready.
