# Haven Paediatric Centre — Staff Safety Culture & Climate Survey

Run by Consult for Africa as part of the diagnostic audit. Based on the AHRQ Hospital Survey on Patient Safety Culture (HSOPS 2.0), adapted for Haven, with facility-specific items added.

---

## For every member of staff

**Your answers are anonymous and confidential.** They go to Consult for Africa, not to Haven management, and are reported only as grouped totals — never in a way that identifies you. There are no right answers. Honesty is the whole point: it is how we make Haven safer and fairer to work in. It takes about 10 minutes.

**How to answer:** for each statement, choose how much you agree — or how often it happens — on a 1–5 scale.

- Agreement: **1 = Strongly disagree · 2 = Disagree · 3 = Neither · 4 = Agree · 5 = Strongly agree**
- Frequency: **1 = Never · 2 = Rarely · 3 = Sometimes · 4 = Most of the time · 5 = Always**
- Every item also has **"Does not apply / Don't know."**

---

### 1. Your area & teamwork
1. Staff in my area treat each other with respect.
2. When the workload is heavy, we work together as a team to get it done.
3. In this area, people support one another.
4. During busy or difficult moments, we have the staff we need to handle the work. *(reverse-scored)*
5. Work pace here is so high it feels unsafe. *(reverse)*

### 2. Communication & openness
6. Staff feel free to question the decisions or actions of those with more authority.
7. Staff speak up if they see something that might negatively affect patient care.
8. Staff are afraid to ask questions when something does not seem right. *(reverse)*
9. When staff make an error, we talk about it so we can learn from it.
10. We are told about changes to procedures and about mistakes that happen here.

### 3. Response to error (non-punitive)
11. Staff feel that their mistakes are held against them. *(reverse)*
12. When an event is reported, it feels like the *person* is written up, not the *problem*. *(reverse)*
13. When we make mistakes, we are treated fairly.
14. This area deals with problem staff or repeated problems constructively, not just punitively.

### 4. Reporting & learning
15. When a mistake is caught and corrected before it reaches a patient, it is reported.
16. When a mistake reaches a patient but causes no harm, it is reported.
17. We are given feedback about changes made because of event reports.
18. After a problem, this area actually changes how it works to prevent it happening again.
19. I know how to report a patient-safety concern here.

### 5. Management & supervisor support for safety
20. My supervisor / matron seriously considers staff suggestions for improving safety.
21. My supervisor / matron overlooks safety problems that happen over and over. *(reverse)*
22. Management provides the resources (staff, equipment, drugs) needed to give safe care.
23. Management's actions show that patient safety is a top priority.
24. Management only seems interested in patient safety after something goes wrong. *(reverse)*

### 6. Handoffs & information
25. Important patient-care information is shared clearly across shifts.
26. Things "fall between the cracks" when patients are handed over between shifts or staff. *(reverse)*
27. Shift changes are a risky time for patients here. *(reverse)*

### 7. Emergency readiness & routines (Haven-specific)
28. The crash cart / emergency tray is fully stocked and ready on every shift.
29. There is a routine, every shift, for checking emergency drugs and equipment.
30. If a child needed emergency resuscitation right now, we have what we need to respond.
31. I have had recent resuscitation training (BLS / PALS / NRP) for the patients I care for.
32. Essential drugs or consumables are out of stock when we need them. *(reverse)*

### 8. Incentives, fairness & ownership (Haven-specific)
33. I understand clearly what I am responsible for and what good performance looks like in my role.
34. The way pay, commission or rewards work here is fair and clear.
35. The rewards and recognition here encourage good, safe, careful work (not just speed or volume).
36. I feel a sense of ownership for how well Haven runs, not just my own tasks.

### 9. Overall
37. Please give Haven an overall grade on patient safety: **Excellent · Very good · Acceptable · Poor · Failing.**
38. I would recommend Haven to friends and family as a place to receive care. *(1–5)*
39. I would recommend Haven to a friend as a place to work. *(1–5)*

### 10. A little about you *(optional — helps us group answers; never used to identify you)*
- Primary area: Nursing · Medical/Clinical · Pharmacy · Front desk / Customer service · Admin/Accounts · Operations · Other
- Do you interact directly with patients? Yes / No
- How long have you worked at Haven? <6 months · 6–12 months · 1–2 years · 2 years+

*We deliberately do not ask for your name, exact role, or shift, so that no answer can be traced to you.*

### 11. Two open questions *(optional)*
- What is the **one thing** that would make Haven safer for patients?
- What is the **one thing** that would make Haven a better place to work?
